# Formatting Rules

The formatting rules clear the small scaffolding that clutters a statement once its shape is settled, each one reaching a single token, gap, or spelling rather than the construct holding it. Each rewrite is narrower than a layout rule and more pervasive than an ordering rule, tidying what the eye reads without touching the structure it reads.
